What this is

We opened Ling the way a new learner does, then checked your paywall against the market.

Adapty powers subscriptions for 30,000+ apps, so we see what moves conversion across the market, not inside one app. We installed Ling, went through onboarding for Arabic, added a second course, reached your paywall, read your App Store listing, and compared every element with State of Paywalls 2026.

What follows is five ideas, not fifteen. Each one is a test you can run, and each one is built into a clickable prototype in your own design. Benchmarks are for Education apps in the US, your largest App Store market.

We did not redesign your paywall

Every element in the prototype is yours: the monkey and globe art, your orange button, the blue trial badge, your plan cards, your timeline and your own prices. Only what sits on the screen, and when, has changed.

You may have tested some of this

The five ideas

Ranked by what we would test first.

Offer changes first, cosmetics last. Each one links straight to the screen in the prototype.

01

Catch the close with a second chance

In our walk the X was on screen from the first second, and we could not see anything after it. Most apps let that person walk away. Bring the 7-day trial back once, on your own EUR 79.99 annual price, shown as EUR 1.54 a week.

📊 State of Paywalls 2026 · Only 4% of apps show a second paywall on close. It beats the main paywall in 45% of apps.
See the screen ARPU +10–15%Based on Adapty data
02

Show the paywall when onboarding ends

Onboarding built us a 7-week Arabic plan, asked for an account and went straight to lessons. The first price appeared only when we added a second course. Put the paywall right after the plan, while the plan is still on screen, with the learner's name in the headline.

📊 State of Paywalls 2026 · Education: onboarding paywalls earn $229 per 1,000 views, later paywalls $73.

03

Put your 4.6 stars and Apple award on the paywall

The paywall rotates four user reviews but shows no star average and no rating count, and one review is about Khmer on a paywall selling Amharic. Your 4.6 from 16K US ratings and Apple's Great Apps for Education 2023 are not there.

📊 State of Paywalls 2026 · Only 27% of top paywalls show social proof; 20% of trial paywalls show awards.

04

Say “no payment now” next to the button

Your Today / Day 5 / Day 7 timeline is good, but it sits one screen before the prices. On the plan screen, “Cancel anytime” is grey fine print under Restore, below the fold. Bring the timeline down to the button.

📊 State of Paywalls 2026 · 74% of top trial paywalls put a reassurance line next to the button.

05

Hold the X for three seconds

The X is the first thing on both trial screens, before the timeline and before the plans. A short delay lets the headline, your rating and the trial land first.
